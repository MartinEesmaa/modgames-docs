# Tux Racer

Tux Racer lets you take the role of Tux Linux Penguin to race in snow covered mountains.

The game files were used for assets and resources: TCL, WAV, IT and RGB (SGI image)

## Music

At music folder, there are five Impulse Tracker audio files.

You can replace original music files easily.

## Sound Effects

At sounds folder, there are 7 WAVE audio files for sound effects.

Please note that it must have WAV stereo channels of 16-bit 44.1 KHZ.
